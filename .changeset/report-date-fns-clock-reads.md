`no-ambient-clock` reports more current-time reads outside the `clock` owners: argument-less `new TZDate()` and `new TZDateMini()` and one-argument `TZDate.tz(zone)` from `@date-fns/tz`, `@date-fns/tz/date` or `@date-fns/tz/date/mini`, and calls to the date-fns helpers that read the clock, such as `constructNow`, `startOfToday`, `isToday`, `isPast`, `isThisWeek`, `formatDistanceToNow` and `isMatch`, whether imported by name, through a namespace, from a `date-fns/<name>` subpath, from `date-fns/fp` once fully applied, or by literal dynamic import. Re-exporting those helpers by name, or with `export * from "date-fns"`, `date-fns/fp` or a helper's subpath, also reports. Parentheses, `as`, `satisfies` and `!` around a read no longer change the result: `(Date.now as typeof Date.now)()` and `Date.now!()` now report, and a provider such as `{ now: () => startOfToday() as Date }` passes. `no-ambient-random` treats `(Math.random as () => number)()` the same way. Projects with a `clock` owner list may see new errors.
